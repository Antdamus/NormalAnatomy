CORE_FACTS_USED:

* Chronic pancreatitis classically shows an atrophic pancreas with diffuse calcifications and a dilated/beaded duct; calcifications in the pancreatic duct distribution are described as pathognomonic.
* Calcified hepatic metastases include mucinous cancers, osteosarcoma, and treated lymphoma.
* Treated or prior fungal/granulomatous infection may leave punctate hepatic calcifications/granulomas.
* Hepatic echinococcal cysts may contain daughter cysts or floating membranes and may demonstrate peripheral calcification.
* Splenic calcification differential includes granulomatous disease, remote infarct, hematoma, calcified splenic artery aneurysm, and cystic splenic lesions.
* Cortical nephrocalcinosis is dystrophic cortical calcification associated with diffuse cortical injury.
