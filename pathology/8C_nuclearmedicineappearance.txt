NUCLEAR MEDICINE APPEARANCE ENCODING RULE (MANDATORY WHEN SOURCE SUPPORTS)

If the provided Core/RadPrimer/STATdx source explicitly describes nuclear medicine appearance
(for example tracer uptake or non-uptake, photopenia, warm-versus-cold behavior, scintigraphic distribution, PET avidity, retention, washout, excretion, phase/timing, biodistribution, tracer-specific discriminator behavior, or source-named nuclear medicine pivots):

You MUST generate at least ONE dedicated High-Yield card encoding that nuclear medicine appearance.

(If Sectioned Build mode is active, this requirement is QUEUED and must be emitted only in SECTION G.)

Rules:
- Nuclear medicine details may be included ONLY if explicitly stated in the source.
- Include the tracer or exam name exactly as written when possible.
- Include uptake, photopenia, avidity, retention, washout, excretion, phase/timing, or physiologic biodistribution only if source-supported.
- Do NOT infer uptake, photopenia, avidity, washout, retention, excretion, timing, or physiologic distribution unless source-supported.
- Do NOT generalize one tracer's behavior to another tracer.
- Do NOT convert source silence into a negative finding.
- Keep the card tight: 2-6 short lines max.

Recommended format:

High_Yield_Q:
"For [ENTITY], what tracer-specific nuclear medicine or PET/SPECT pattern should be recognized?"

High_Yield_A:
- Tracer / exam: [source-stated tracer or study]
- Appearance: [source-stated uptake, photopenia, avidity, retention, washout, excretion, distribution, or other behavior]
- Timing / phase: [only if explicitly stated]
- Imaging specificity: [Diagnostic / Strongly suggestive / Nonspecific / Requires correlation, only when relevant]
- Recognition pivot: [one line: why this helps recognize or discriminate]

If the nuclear medicine description is too long:
- Split into multiple High-Yield cards ONLY if each card is its own distinct exam pivot.
- Otherwise omit lower-value details.
